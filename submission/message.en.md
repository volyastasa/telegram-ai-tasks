**Tasks — keep the conversation with the task**

Important commitments often get buried in chats. Tasks brings them into one place while keeping the discussion that explains them close at hand.

The interactive demo shows a new Tasks section alongside chats: a unified list with assignees, due dates and statuses. Each task keeps its relevant discussion, lets you jump to the exact source message, and includes flows for creating a task from a message and sharing it with its assignee.

Try it: [tasks.rifeygold.com](https://tasks.rifeygold.com/)

Source (MIT): [volyastasa/telegram-ai-tasks](https://github.com/volyastasa/telegram-ai-tasks)

This is a standalone HTML/CSS/JavaScript browser prototype with desktop and iPhone layouts. It uses synthetic chats and prepared analysis results; sharing is simulated locally. It does not connect to a Telegram account or an AI service.

Attached: **SOURCE.zip** with the runnable project and **desktop.mp4** / **iphone.mp4**, two 81-second guided demonstrations.
